# Example Storyboard Scenes
# One scene per line

Scene 1: Jaja is happily planting colorful flowers in a beautiful garden with butterflies flying around
Scene 2: Tutu appears from behind a large oak tree, looking curious and slightly mischievous
Scene 3: They discover a glowing magical portal hidden behind the garden waterfall
Scene 4: Both characters hold hands and step through the shimmering portal together
Scene 5: They arrive in a colorful fantasy world with floating islands and rainbow rivers
Scene 6: Meeting friendly woodland creatures - rabbits, deer, and birds gather around them
Scene 7: Exploring a crystal cave with glowing blue and purple minerals everywhere
Scene 8: Finding an ancient treasure chest covered in moss and vines
Scene 9: Opening the chest to reveal magical items - a glowing wand and golden wings
Scene 10: Jaja uses the magic wand to help injured animals in the forest
Scene 11: Tutu wears the golden wings and flies gracefully through the clouds
Scene 12: Both characters dance together in a magical rainbow waterfall
Scene 13: Watching a beautiful orange and pink sunset over the fantasy landscape
Scene 14: Returning through the portal back to their garden, carrying magical flowers
Scene 15: Waving goodbye to their new fantasy friends, promising to return someday
